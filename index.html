🎙️ Hey Friday — Voice AI

Not listening

Say “Hey Friday”, then ask your question. Friday can answer NCERT and general questions, and read the answer aloud.

How to use

1. Add your Gemini API key below. 2. Tap “Enable Hey Friday”. 3. Allow microphone access. 4. Say “Hey Friday, explain Newton’s laws” or say “Hey Friday” and then ask your question.

Voice recognition needs a supported browser such as Chrome and microphone permission. Keep Friday open while using wake-word listening.
Privacy: your spoken question is sent to Gemini to generate an answer. Do not paste a key on a public/shared device. This browser-only setup is for personal use; a secure server is safer for a public website.
